## Accepted judgement calls

- **Replay naming kept distinct** (round 4, from R1-14). The replay types
  keep their separate names (`ReplayChild` for the process, `ReplayResult`
  for the settled outcome) rather than collapsing into one; each is
  documented against the other (1459e90d, 40955ca5).
- **Exit channels stay on their frames** (round 5, R5-01). The walker's nine
  exit channels remain owned by their loop, call and frame groups
  (`LoopExits`, `CallExits`, `FrameExits`), each bound to the walk's one
  clock, rather than moving to a central exit board that owns the clock
  (b16ef6d3).
- **A trusted coverage failure is detected as uncovered plus `trusted(f)`**
  (round 6, R6-02). There is no separate status for it: `severity` reads
  `not covered` together with `trusted(f)` as `skCoverageFailure`, the
  highest rank, so no weaker finding displaces it (51d6a02b).
